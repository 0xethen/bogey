# For participants

A short guide to taking part, written so an organizer can send it around or print it.

## Before the event

1. **Register.** Use the link the organizers sent, or find the event under **Events**. You sign in with your email: Bogey sends a
   link and a six-digit code, so there is no password to remember. Answer the questions; some ask for a form or a file.
2. **Wait for approval** if the event reviews registrations. You'll get an email when it's decided, and the bell in the header
   shows it too. If the event is full you may be on a waitlist; you move up by yourself when a place opens.
3. **Make a team** on the **Team** tab (or join one with a team code), or go solo if the event allows it. Don't know anyone?
   The Team tab's board lets you say what you're good at, ask to join teams that are looking, and accept invitations. Organizers
   may also place people who are still without a team.
4. **Add the event to your calendar** under Account → Calendar if you like. Times show in your timezone unless the event shows its own.

## On the day

- **Check in** at the desk: show the QR code on your **badge** (on the event home), or type the eight-character code under it. Some
  events let you check yourself in with a code on a screen.
- The **Agenda** tab lists sessions. When sign-ups are open, pick the sessions you want; some slots let you choose one.
- Ask the organizers a question on the **Messages** tab. They can answer you or everyone.

## Submitting

On the **Submission** tab, fill in the form your team was given: a title, descriptions, links, files. Anyone on the team can edit it.
It goes in steps: **Project** (the form), **Prizes** (when there are prizes to enter) and **Review**. **Next** saves the draft as you go.
Review shows what judges see first (the title, your team, its division and how much is filled in), what's still missing, and whether
the project shows in the public gallery. When the event lets teams pick their division, you can move your whole team there too.
It saves as a draft until the team presses **Submit** on Review; if the submissions window closes first, drafts are submitted for you
when the organizers have that switched on. Some forms are only for certain divisions; you only see the ones for yours.

You can change a submission until its window closes. After that it's read only.

## After

When results are published, the **Results** tab shows awards and, if the organizers share them, judges' feedback and your scores.
Certificates, if there are any, are at `/events/<event>/certificates`. If the instance turns on public pages, your shared projects, awards and events can
live on your own page at `/@<handle>` (Account → Profile).

## If something goes wrong

Tell the organizers on **Messages**. If you can't sign in, ask for a new link; if the page says a window is closed, it is, and the
organizers are the only ones who can open it again.
