# For staff

Staff are the volunteers who run the day: they check people in, take attendance and answer questions. They see the event's people but can't
change its settings.

## The check-in desk

Open the **Check-in** tab.

- **Scan** the QR code on a badge with the camera, or **type** the eight-character code under it, or **search** by name or email.
- If the event asks for it, **confirm the name** on screen against the person's ID before you check them in.
- **Undo** a check-in from the recent list if you got the wrong person; **check out** someone who is leaving for good.
- With walk-ins switched on (Manage → Registration → Check-in), you can register someone who isn't on the roster and check them in at once.
- The counts show who has arrived, by role and division. The **venue code** on that page is for a screen: people enter it on the event home to check themselves in, when self check-in is on.

## Attendance at sessions

Sessions that take attendance show a code and a roster. Mark people present from the roster, or let them enter the session's code themselves.
Attendance can be exported as a CSV from the agenda's session list by organizers.

## Messages

Participants' questions arrive on the **Messages** tab. Answer the ones you can; leave the rest for the organizers. An answer that helps everyone
can be shared with everyone.

## What you can't do

Staff can read the event's people, check people in and take attendance. Changing settings, teams, scores or results needs an organizer.
